# Phase 2 — Market Intelligence and Paper Trading

Phase 2 extends the Phase 1 foundation with provider-neutral market contracts, sample-data adapters, explainable signal contracts, paper-order validation and execution simulation, watchlists, alerts, analytics, chart components and user-facing workspaces.

## Explicit boundaries
- No broker credentials or live order placement.
- No claim of live or exchange-certified market data.
- Sample/delayed provenance is visible in UI and API payloads.
- Probability is educational decision support, never a guarantee.
- Persistent production storage and external market providers are connected in later phases.
